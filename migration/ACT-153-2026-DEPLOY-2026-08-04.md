# Act 153-2026 — Revocable Trusts: deploy notes (2026-08-04)

## What this is

Act 153-2026 (originating as P. del S. 773) was signed by the Governor on **July 30, 2026** and
takes effect **January 30, 2027**. It amends 44 articles of the Puerto Rico Trust Act
(Law 219-2012), expressly recognizes the revocable trust, and **reverses the irrevocability
presumption**: from January 30, 2027, a deed silent on revocability is presumed *revocable*.

Riefkohl Law has first-party standing on this reform — the September 2025 column that argued for
it, and written comments submitted to the House Judiciary Committee in May 2026 on Articles 10
and 46, both of which the enacted text tracks. That is the strongest E-E-A-T signal the trust
cluster has, and both new pages state it plainly.

Source of truth for the content: the firm's own client alert of August 4, 2026, cross-checked
article by article against the enrolled bill text.
