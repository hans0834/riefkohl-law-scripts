How Much Does a Declaratoria de Herederos Cost in Puerto Rico? (And Is There a "Free" Option?)

A declaratoria de herederos (declaration of heirs) is the legal process that formally establishes who the legal heirs of a person who died without a will are under Puerto Rico law. It is often the first step families need before they can transfer, sell, or retitle a deceased loved one's property. If you have been searching for what a declaratoria de herederos costs, or whether you can get one for free, here is an honest, plain-language overview.

What Drives the Cost and Complexity

There is no single flat price that applies to every case, because the work involved varies. The main factors are the route your case takes and the circumstances of the estate. There are two general paths: a notarial route, which is handled before a notary and is typically faster and less costly when heirs agree; and a judicial route, which goes through the court and is usually necessary when there is a dispute, a minor heir, or other complications. Costs and timelines generally rise with the number of heirs and whether real property (a home, land, or other titled assets) is involved and needs to be retitled.

Qué Determina el Costo y la Complejidad

No existe un precio único que aplique a todos los casos, porque el trabajo requerido varía. Los factores principales son la vía que toma su caso y las circunstancias de la herencia. Existen dos vías generales: la vía notarial, que se tramita ante un notario y suele ser más rápida y económica cuando los herederos están de acuerdo; y la vía judicial, que se tramita ante el tribunal y por lo general es necesaria cuando hay una disputa, un heredero menor de edad u otras complicaciones. Los costos y los plazos generalmente aumentan según el número de herederos y si hay bienes inmuebles (una casa, un terreno u otros bienes titulados) que deban cambiar de título.
